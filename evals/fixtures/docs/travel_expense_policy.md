# Travel and Entertainment Policy — Tessera Devices

*Fixture document. Deliberately mirrors the shape of Meridian's policy so the same
rule extraction is exercised, with thresholds chosen to make the expected answer
countable by hand.*

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to travel and entertainment accounts only: airfare, hotels and
meals. It does not apply to payroll, freight or any other operating expense.

## 2. Approval

Any travel or entertainment charge of **USD 150 or more** requires a documented
approval reference recorded against the transaction. Charges below that threshold
do not require one.

## 3. Currency

Where a charge is incurred in a currency other than the entity's reporting
currency, the local equivalent at the month-end rate applies for the purposes of
testing this threshold.

## 4. Headcount

Headcount and full-time-equivalent reporting is produced by the People team in the
HR system and is **not maintained in the finance ledger**.
